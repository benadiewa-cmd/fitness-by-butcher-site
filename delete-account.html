Delete your Prodigy Fitness account

This page is for the Prodigy Fitness app, built by Willie "the Butcher" Benadie.

In the app

  1. Open Prodigy Fitness and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email benadiewa@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your login is deleted. If you created your family, everyone in it is deleted too, with all workouts, food logs, measurements, progress photos, medication and bloodwork records, coach links, chat messages, training calendar entries and (for coaches) your client list. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires it.

You can also delete single entries or family members at any time without deleting your account.
