Delete your Fitness By Butcher account

This page is for the Fitness By Butcher app by Willie Benadie.

In the app

  1. Open Fitness By Butcher and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email benadiewa@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your login is deleted. If you created your family, everyone in it is deleted too, with all workouts, food logs, measurements, progress photos, medication and bloodwork records, coach links and chat messages. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires it.

You can also delete single entries or family members at any time without deleting your account.
