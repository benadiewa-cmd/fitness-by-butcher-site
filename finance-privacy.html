Finance By Butcher

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Finance By Butcher collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your own phone, and the app has no way to send it anywhere.

What the app collects

Nothing. No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, no tracking of any kind.

Where your information is kept

Everything you type into the app — members, subscriptions, payments, minutes, diary entries and any files you add — is written to storage belonging to the app on your own device. It is not sent to the developer, and it is not sent to anybody else.

Internet access

The app makes no network requests. Its pages are loaded from inside the app itself rather than from a website, and there is no server for it to talk to. The Android build carries the ordinary internet permission that almost every app carries, but nothing is sent out and nothing is fetched in.

Backups you make yourself

The app can write a backup of your books to a file. Where that file goes, and who you send it to, is entirely your choice and your responsibility. The developer never receives it.

Children

The app is intended for adults keeping the books of a club. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anybody.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything it holds. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to request the deletion of and nobody to request it from.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this, the policy will be updated here and the date at the top will change.
