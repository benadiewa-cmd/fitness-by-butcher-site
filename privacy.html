Prodigy Fitness Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what Prodigy Fitness ("the app", "we"; built by Willie "the Butcher" Benadie) collects, why, and your choices. The app is operated by Willie Benadie, South Africa. Contact: benadiewa@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Family profiles: names you enter, plus each person's optional age, sex, height, weight, activity level and fitness goals.
  • Training data: workouts, sets, reps, weights, notes and routines.
  • Nutrition data: food diary entries, saved foods and water intake.
  • Body data: measurements (weight, body fat, waist and so on) and progress photos you choose to take or upload.
  • Medication and bloodwork (optional): medicines, doses and schedules you enter as prescribed by your doctor, injection sites, and blood-test results. The app only records what you enter; it never suggests a dose.
  • Coaching (optional): if you link to a coach, the plans the coach sets, your results, and chat messages between you and the coach.
  • Training calendar (optional): the dates, times and names of training sessions you or your coach plan.
  • Health Connect (optional, Android): if you switch it on, the app reads your daily steps, floors climbed, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, weight, blood pressure and blood glucose from Google Health Connect, where apps such as Garmin Connect, Samsung Health or Fitbit, smart scales, blood pressure cuffs and glucose meters share them. The app uses them to show your activity, sleep and readiness, adjust your calorie target and fill in your check-ins. Each day's steps, sleep time, resting heart rate, HRV and readiness score are saved in your private daily log so your trends, family challenges and reports work. Workout heart rate is saved with the workout, weigh-ins go to your Body log and blood pressure and glucose readings you choose to add go to your private vitals log. The app only reads from Health Connect, never writes to it, and you can switch each item off in the app or remove access in Health Connect at any time.
  • Daily health log (optional): your morning check-in (mood, energy, stress, soreness), the habits you tick, and blood pressure and glucose readings you enter. Private to your family; coaches never see them.
  • Recipes, meal plans and family challenges (optional): recipes and weekly meal plans you create, and challenges set for your family.
  • GPS runs, walks and rides (optional): only while the GPS tracker screen is open and only after you allow location, the app uses your phone's location to measure distance, pace and the route. The distance, time and splits are saved with the workout; the route map is saved in a separate record private to your family (friends and coaches never see it). The app never tracks your location in the background.
  • Fasting, supplements and Chair Tai Chi (optional): your fasting times, the supplements you list with how much is left, and your Chair Tai Chi progress and the limits you tick (such as back problems). Private to your family.
  • InBody and similar scans (optional): the numbers from a body composition scan you photograph or type in (weight, muscle, body fat, visceral fat and so on), saved with your body measurements.
  • Coach notes and templates (coaches only): dated notes a coach writes about a client's progress and the programme templates a coach saves. Only that coach can see them; the client never does.
  • Health tracker (optional): blood test results you type in or photograph (cholesterol, blood sugar, kidney, liver, thyroid and so on), your 7-day blood pressure check, everyday medicines and when they were taken, family appointments, caffeine and alcohol you log, a symptom and mood diary, sleep goals, monthly fitness test results and VO2 max, menstrual cycle tracking, and an emergency card (blood type, allergies, conditions, medicines, doctor, medical aid and emergency contacts). All private to your family; coaches and friends never see them. If you switch on "Show on the lock screen" for the emergency card, its details are visible on your phone's lock screen to anyone holding the phone.
  • Meal plans (optional): a meal plan you upload (PDF, photo or text) or one the AI makes for you, and the preferences you give (style, allergies, dislikes).
  • Doctor share links (optional): when you make one, a long random link shows the parts you choose (blood pressure, glucose, blood tests, medicines, weight) read-only in a browser until it expires (at most 31 days) or you switch it off. Anyone you give the link to can open it while it works. We record when it was first opened.
  • Pregnancy, postpartum and children's growth (optional): due or birth date, weight before pregnancy, breastfeeding, and children's date of birth, height and weight. Private to your family.
  • Friend challenges (optional): friends who join a challenge see each participant's name and total (workouts, active minutes or kg lifted) for the challenge dates. Nothing else is shared.
  • Coach business (coaches): packages, prices, invoice numbers, payment status and the coach's own payment link; weekly check-in questions and the client's answers; the coach's logo, colour and tagline (shown to their clients). Payments happen on the coach's own payment provider (such as PayFast or Yoco); the app never handles card details.
  • Bluetooth heart-rate straps (optional): live heart rate while connected; the average and maximum are saved with the workout. Bluetooth isn't used for anything else and not for location.
  • Imported activities and Strava (optional): GPX/TCX files you choose, or, once you connect Strava, your recent activities (name, type, time, distance, heart rate and route). Strava access can be removed in the app or in Strava at any time. The app never posts to Strava.
  • Apple Health (iPhone, optional): the same kinds of readings as Health Connect on Android (steps, sleep, heart rate, HRV, weight, blood pressure, glucose, VO2 max), read only, never written, never used for advertising.
  • Form check (optional): 12 still frames from a video you choose are sent to the AI for that check; the video itself isn't uploaded or stored.
  • Family alerts: when a very high blood pressure or a very low or high blood sugar is recorded, the other adults in your family get a push notification. Nothing is sent to anyone outside your family.
  • Reminders: session, meal, rest-timer and check-in reminders are scheduled on your phone itself; nothing about them is sent to us.
  • Coach client list (coaches only): if you use Coach mode, the names, phone numbers, email addresses, contact type, start and end dates and notes you enter about your clients. Only you can see your client list. Enter only details your clients have agreed to share with you.
  • Social data: friend connections, likes, comments, blocks and reports.

We don't collect your location except while you use the GPS tracker, we don't collect your contacts, and we don't use advertising or tracking. Some of your data is also kept on your phone so the app works without a connection; it is removed when you sign out or uninstall.

How we use it

We use your data only to provide the app's features: building plans, tracking progress, calculating targets, showing your family's data to your family, and showing shared workouts to friends you accept.

Who can see your data

  • Your family: people who joined your family with its family code can see and edit everyone in that family.
  • Friends: accepted friends see the workouts (exercise names, sets, weights, notes) of a family member who has "share with friends" turned on. They never see food, body measurements or photos. You can turn sharing off per person in Settings.
  • Your coach (if you link one): sees the plans and workout results of the clients they manage, and your chat messages. Progress photos are off by default. A coach never sees medication, bloodwork, blood pressure, glucose, sleep, your daily check-ins, supplements, fasting, Chair Tai Chi limits, GPS routes, appointments, medicines, caffeine, alcohol, your diary, cycle tracking or your emergency card; that stays private to your family. You can choose to send your coach a health report yourself. A coach can see and book sessions in your training calendar.
  • Service providers:
  • Supabase hosts our database and photo storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): only when you use an AI feature, what that feature needs is sent to Anthropic to answer that one request: a meal description or photo, a training-plan file, a quick-log sentence, an InBody result sheet photo, two progress photos you ask it to compare, or, for "Ask the coach" and the weekly summary, a short text summary of your own workouts, food, weight, sleep, habits and goals. Medication, bloodwork, blood pressure and glucose are never sent with these. When you choose to import or explain a blood test, the report or results are sent for that request; when you ask the AI to build a meal plan, your targets, preferences and short health notes (for example "blood pressure is raised") are sent, never the raw readings or your name. It's used only to answer that request.
  • Open Food Facts: barcode numbers you scan are looked up in this public food database.
  • We don't sell your data.

Children

Children can be added as family members by a parent or guardian, who manages their data. Children under 13 shouldn't create their own account. A parent can delete a child's profile and data at any time (Family → Edit → Remove).

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete individual entries, family members, or your whole account in the app (Settings → Delete account). If you can't use the app, email benadiewa@gmail.com from your account's email address and we'll delete your account and data within 30 days. Deleting the account that created a family permanently deletes the whole family's data, including photos.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by access rules, so each family can only reach its own records. Progress photos are stored privately and shown through short-lived links.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under POPIA in South Africa or the GDPR in Europe), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. Email benadiewa@gmail.com.

Health Connect and Apple Health

Data the app reads from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone) is used only to show you your activity, sleep, heart rate, readiness, weight and vitals in the app, to set your calorie target, and to build your own check-ins, challenges and reports. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except people you choose in the app (your family, and your coach for workout results if you link one). Prodigy Fitness's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Health information

The app gives general fitness and nutrition information. It isn't medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet plan.
