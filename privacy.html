Fitness By Butcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what Fitness By Butcher ("the app", "we") collects, why, and your choices. The app is operated by Willie Benadie, South Africa. Contact: benadiewa@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Family profiles: names you enter, plus each person's optional age, sex, height, weight, activity level and fitness goals.
  • Training data: workouts, sets, reps, weights, notes and routines.
  • Nutrition data: food diary entries, saved foods and water intake.
  • Body data: measurements (weight, body fat, waist and so on) and progress photos you choose to take or upload.
  • Medication and bloodwork (optional): medicines, doses and schedules you enter as prescribed by your doctor, injection sites, and blood-test results. The app only records what you enter; it never suggests a dose.
  • Coaching (optional): if you link to a coach, the plans the coach sets, your results, and chat messages between you and the coach.
  • Social data: friend connections, likes, comments, blocks and reports.

We don't collect your location or contacts, and we don't use advertising or tracking.

How we use it

We use your data only to provide the app's features: building plans, tracking progress, calculating targets, showing your family's data to your family, and showing shared workouts to friends you accept.

Who can see your data

  • Your family: people who joined your family with its family code can see and edit everyone in that family.
  • Friends: accepted friends see the workouts (exercise names, sets, weights, notes) of a family member who has "share with friends" turned on. They never see food, body measurements or photos. You can turn sharing off per person in Settings.
  • Your coach (if you link one): sees the plans and workout results of the clients they manage, and your chat messages. Progress photos are off by default. A coach never sees medication or bloodwork; that stays private to your family.
  • Service providers:
  • Supabase hosts our database and photo storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): when you choose an AI food feature and agree, the meal description or photo you provide is sent to Anthropic to estimate nutrition. It's used only to answer that request.
  • Open Food Facts: barcode numbers you scan are looked up in this public food database.
  • We don't sell your data.

Children

Children can be added as family members by a parent or guardian, who manages their data. Children under 13 shouldn't create their own account. A parent can delete a child's profile and data at any time (Family → Edit → Remove).

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete individual entries, family members, or your whole account in the app (Settings → Delete account). If you can't use the app, email benadiewa@gmail.com from your account's email address and we'll delete your account and data within 30 days. Deleting the account that created a family permanently deletes the whole family's data, including photos.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by access rules, so each family can only reach its own records. Progress photos are stored privately and shown through short-lived links.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under POPIA in South Africa or the GDPR in Europe), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. Email benadiewa@gmail.com.

Health information

The app gives general fitness and nutrition information. It isn't medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet plan.

Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the date above.
